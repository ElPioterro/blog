# About ElPioTerro's Transcription Blog

I'm a musician and music educator passionate about breaking down great solos to understand what makes them work. This blog serves as both my personal collection and a resource for other musicians looking to study these solos.

## My Transcription Process

Each transcription on this site follows a rigorous process:

1. **Deep listening** - I listen to the solo dozens of times to internalize it
2. **Slow-motion transcription** - Using software to slow down difficult passages
3. **Notation** - Creating accurate sheet music in MuseScore
4. **Analysis** - Breaking down the harmonic and rhythmic concepts
5. **Practice Tips** - Developing exercises to help internalize the solo

## Why I Created This Site

As a musician, I've always learned the most from transcribing and analyzing the work of masters. This site helps me organize my transcriptions and share them with fellow musicians. I believe that understanding what makes a great solo "work" is invaluable for developing your own musical voice.
