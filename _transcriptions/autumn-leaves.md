## About This Transcription

This transcription covers Chet Baker's melodic trumpet solo on the jazz standard "Autumn Leaves" from his album "Chet Baker Sings" (1956). This solo exemplifies Baker's lyrical, singing approach to improvisation.

Baker's solo follows the chord changes closely, emphasizing chord tones with occasional chromatic approaches. His lines are predominantly diatonic with thoughtful use of the minor ii-V-i progression.
